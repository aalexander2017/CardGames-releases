## Installing on a phone or Fire tablet

1. Open the **Download** link for an app on the phone or tablet.
2. When the download finishes, tap the file to open it.
3. If asked, allow your browser or Files app to **install unknown apps**, then go back and tap **Install**.
4. To update, install the new version over the old one. Your games are kept.

Apps whose names end in **-Classic** are for older Fire tablets (Fire OS 5 and 6). Use the others on everything else.

These are test builds, signed with a test key, for installing on your own devices.
